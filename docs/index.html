How It Works

From browser tab graveyard to scheduled calendar focus blocks in 3 steps.

01 / CAPTURE

Drop & Forget

Drop any URL or PDF into the desktop bin. Works via drag-and-drop, global keyboard hotkey, or CLI.

02 / EXTRACT & SCHEDULE

Autonomous Booking

Jarvis estimates reading time, extracts webinar schedules, normalizes timezones (e.g. EDT ➔ local), and reserves a collision-free calendar slot.

03 / FOCUS

Slot-Is-Up Brief

When your slot begins, receive a desktop notification and a high-contrast takeaway brief in your inbox ready for deep work.
