// How it works

Three simple steps.

01
Drop it.
Drag any link, selected text, or file straight into the floating bin.
02
Pick a time.
Choose in two hours, tomorrow morning, next Monday, or pick a date.
03
Get reminded.
A desktop alert rings at your computer, or an email arrives in your inbox.
// Built right

Fast, light, and private.

Desktop and Email
Alerts pop up on your screen. Curated briefings go straight to your email inbox.
macOS and Linux
Native system daemons. Starts instantly without heavy browser runtimes.
7.8 MB Memory
Built in compiled Rust. Uses almost zero CPU while waiting for drops.
100% Private
No user accounts. No tracking. Your drops stay safely on your machine.
