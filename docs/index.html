How It Works
PIPELINE MANIFEST // 01-03
STEP 01

Frictionless Ingestion

Drag links, PDFs, or highlighted text into the desktop bin, hit your global hotkey Super+Shift+F, or pipe from the command line with forwardbin add.

STEP 02

Deep Content & Timezone Logic

Jarvis parses hidden event dates, estimates reading duration, deterministically converts foreign timezones (e.g. EDT ➔ local IST), and finds an open, collision-free slot in your calendar schedule.

STEP 03

Focus Chimes & Inbox Briefs

The moment your scheduled slot arrives, Jarvis triggers a desktop alert and delivers a clean, high-contrast Gmail briefing with key takeaways and direct source links ready for deep focus.

Floating Desktop Drop Bin

Minimalist, always-on-top translucent bin with realistic physics. Resides unobtrusively on your screen.

Queue Dashboard & Briefings

Click the bin to inspect pending reading slots, reschedule sessions, or open materials directly.
